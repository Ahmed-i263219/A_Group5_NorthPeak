7-Day Hunza, Attabad & Khunjerab Expedition

Complete guided mountain tour from Islamabad covering Karimabad, Baltit Fort, Attabad Lake, Hussaini Bridge, and Khunjerab Pass with verified accommodations.

Max Altitude 4,693m (Khunjerab Pass)

Expedition Overview

Designed for university groups and families seeking safe, transparent mountain travel, this expedition pairs comfortable valley lodges with calibrated daily driving distances and certified Karakoram drivers.

  • Verified heritage & valley-view lodges
  • Daily breakfast & traditional dinner included

High mountain passes reach elevations where oxygen levels drop noticeably. Hydrate regularly, carry warm windproof layers even in summer, and register an emergency contact with our Gilgit Basecamp Desk prior to departure.
